# Incubators & Allies for Doikayt Mobilization Labs

Fit assessed against Doikayt's current status: sole proprietorship, fiscal sponsorship pending.

## Candidates

### [Mozilla Foundation Incubator](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-do/grantmaking/incubator/)
- **Fit:** Strongest match for open-source civic tooling.
- Recruits projects through themed open calls.
- Build Track is for teams with working technology and early users.
- Provides funding, mentorship, and community connections.
- Its [Democracy x AI cohort](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-do/grantmaking/incubator/democracy-ai-cohort/) included a volunteer-run, open-source government-spending tracker that grew out of a grassroots movement.
- **Action:** Watch for the next themed call.

### [Fast Forward](https://www.ffwd.org/accelerator)
- **Fit:** Good, but probably blocked for now.
- Gives a $25,000 grant, a cohort, and mentors.
- Requires registration as a nonprofit in your country.
- 2027 cohort applications closed September 7, 2026.
- **Action:** Ask whether fiscally sponsored projects qualify. The realistic target is the 2028 cohort.

### [The Movement Cooperative](https://movementcooperative.org/)
- **Fit:** Partner or distribution channel, not an incubator.
- A member cooperative of 90+ national and state organizations plus 1,400+ affiliates.
- Members voted in 2021 to prioritize values-aligned and nonprofit technology.
- Issued a call in early 2025 for help developing CRM systems.
- **Action:** Explore partnership or adoption of Doikayt tools by members.

### [May First Movement Technology](https://mayfirst.coop/)
- **Fit:** Peer and possible ally.
- A movement tech cooperative with about 850 member organizations in the US and Mexico as of 2024.
- Relies exclusively on commons-based software.
- **Action:** Consider membership or collaboration.

### [Higher Ground Labs](https://highergroundlabs.com/)
- **Fit:** Poor.
- Invests only in for-profit companies with a path to returns.
- Has moved from broad accelerator cohorts to backing a small number of targeted tools.

### [Tech for Palestine](https://techforpalestine.org)
- **Fit:** Conditional.
- Applies only if a Doikayt tool serves Palestine solidarity organizing, such as protest tools or boycott scaling.
