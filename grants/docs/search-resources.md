# Grant search resources (meta-tools, not funders)

Not funders themselves — tools/services for *finding* funders. Surfaced 2026-08-24 from
content on [thenopi.org](https://thenopi.org) (exact page URL to confirm) while researching NOPI
as a fiscal sponsorship candidate; captured here since it's grant-search infrastructure, not
fiscal-sponsorship-specific.

## Paid grant databases

- **GrantStation** — subscription grant database/platform. Cheapest when purchased through a
  nonprofit association membership or through TechSoup, which also runs an annual sale with
  deeper discounts. **Action:** check current TechSoup pricing/sale timing before buying direct.
- **Foundation Directory Online (FDO)** — free when accessed through a participating public
  library or community center; paid otherwise. **Action:** check whether a local library
  (Chris's home area) is an FDO-participating location before paying for a subscription.

## Free resources
